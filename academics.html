About Horizon Academy

Education That Inspires,
Challenges, and Transforms

For over 30 years, we have been building a community of thinkers, innovators, and leaders who go on to shape the world.

Our Main Campus
Established 1994 · 12 Acres
30+
Years of Excellence

Horizon Academy is more than a school — it's a place where students discover who they are and who they want to become. Our holistic approach integrates academics, arts, sports, and character-building.

🔬

World-Class Facilities

State-of-the-art science labs, a fully equipped library, innovation hub, and sports complex designed for 21st-century learning.

🌍

Global Mindset

Exchange programs with 15 countries, Model UN, and an internationally diverse student body that prepares students for a global future.

💡

Innovation & Creativity

Our maker-spaces, coding clubs, and design thinking curriculum help students solve real-world problems from day one.
